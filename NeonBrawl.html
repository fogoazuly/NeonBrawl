NeonBrawl 3D — Free Arena Survival Shooter with Co-op & Roguelite Upgrades

NeonBrawl 3D is a free wave-survival arena shooter in your browser — a neon roguelite where every run builds a different overpowered kit. Survive escalating waves, fight bosses, and stack skill cards: orbiting orbs, chain lightning, summons, spells, melee builds, drones, turrets and dozens more.

Play solo or in online co-op with friends via room code. Between runs, spend gold on permanent upgrades and unlock new guns — from shotguns and miniguns to flamethrowers, lasers and railguns — plus melee weapons with their own skill tree.

Features

  • Wave survival with mini-objectives, elite enemies and boss fights
  • 100+ skill cards across bullets, melee, orbs, spells, summons, movement and survival
  • Arsenal of unlockable weapons: SMGs, snipers, flamethrower, laser, railgun and more
  • Online co-op multiplayer via room code — free, no account needed
  • Permanent progression: gold, shop upgrades, weapon chests and cosmetics
  • Runs in any modern browser on desktop and mobile — no download

How to play

Move with WASD, aim with the mouse and hold left click to fire. Press F for melee, Space to dash, E to interact, and pick a skill card every level-up. Press Escape to pause. On phones you get dual joysticks (move + aim/fire) with on-screen dash, melee, use and pause buttons.

Is NeonBrawl 3D free to play?

Yes. NeonBrawl 3D is completely free in your browser — no download, no install and no sign-up needed.

Can I play NeonBrawl co-op with friends?

Yes. Host a room, share the room code, and friends join your survival run online for free.

Is NeonBrawl a roguelite?

Yes. Each run you draft skill cards that stack into unique builds, while gold and shop upgrades persist between runs.

Does NeonBrawl work on mobile?

Yes. It runs in mobile browsers with touch controls, though a desktop browser gives the smoothest experience.
